01 — About

Who I Am

I'm a Full-Stack Software Developer with around three years of professional experience building and maintaining enterprise applications across both the public and private sector.

My primary stack is Java with Spring Boot on the backend and Angular with TypeScript on the frontend. I've developed REST APIs and the interfaces that consume them, integrated relational and NoSQL databases through Spring Data JPA and Hibernate, and implemented authentication with Spring Security, JWT and directory integration through LDAP and Active Directory.

I work end-to-end: gathering requirements with product owners and business analysts, delivering functionality in an Agile team, supporting deployment and CI/CD pipelines, and helping troubleshoot and support production systems once they are live.

I care about clean, maintainable code, sensible architecture, performance and security — and I keep learning, because the technology landscape does not stand still.
